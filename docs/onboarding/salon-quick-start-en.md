# WETOP salon quick start: what we need from you

Fill in the attached spreadsheet `salon-quick-start-en.xlsx` (6 tabs). This is the minimum to start taking bookings
on day one. Reminders, loyalty, AI assistant, website and stock can be set up later.

## 1. Salon

1. Salon name.
2. City and full address, map link.
3. Phone and email for clients.
4. Opening hours for each day of the week.
5. Currency (for Qatar: QAR) and languages you speak with clients.

## 2. Payments

Tell us which methods you accept:

- **Cash**
- **Card** (POS terminal, including Apple Pay / Google Pay)
- **Bank transfer** (to the company or to a personal account)
- **Other** (payment link, wallet app): please name it
- Gift certificates: yes or no
- Deposit or prepayment for a booking: yes or no, how much
- Cash in the till on the start day

## 3. Administrator (also the cashier)

- Full name, email for sign-in, phone.
- Can they refund money to a client? Can they see the revenue of the whole salon?
- Owner or manager: name and email, if they will also sign in.

## 4. Specialists (one row each)

- Full name, phone.
- Email for sign-in (optional: needed if the specialist will see their own bookings).
- Services they do.
- Working days and hours.

## 5. Services (one row each)

Category, service name, duration in minutes, price.

## 6. Extras

Drinks, snacks, products: free or paid, and the price.

## What happens next (about one hour)

1. We create your salon: name, country, currency, time zone.
2. Branch: address, phone, opening hours.
3. Services and prices.
4. Specialists: services and working hours.
5. Payment methods.
6. Everyone gets an invitation by email and sets their own password.
7. Test booking together: create, pay, cancel.

## Rules

- Each person has their own email for sign-in.
- Do not send us passwords, ID documents or card numbers.
- Photos and personal details of specialists are published only with their consent.
